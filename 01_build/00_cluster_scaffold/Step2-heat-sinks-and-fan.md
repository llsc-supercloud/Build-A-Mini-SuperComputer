## Optional Scaffolding Steps

There are a few optional steps that you may want to consider completing before putting your boards back into the rack.

**Heat sinks**
  
The rack kit comes with heat sinks for the Raspberry Pi computers.  If you wish to add them, now is the time.  We recommend aligning them so that the channels are aligned with the airflow from the fan

**Fans**
  
There are 2 levels for the fans, high and low speed.  The highspeed fan is quite noisy, and may not be necessary for the applications you are considering for your cluster.  Follow the instructions included in the BrambleBox kit to determine which pins to use for the fans
